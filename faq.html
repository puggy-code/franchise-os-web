Questions? Here are the answers.

Two Hour Maids sends a team of two to clean your home, on your schedule. Here is what people ask us most.

Who comes to my home?

A team of two people. Every team member is background checked, insured and trained by us. We teach the same method every time, so your clean looks the same whoever shows up.

What do you use to clean and sanitize?

Each team brings its own kit, with fresh rags, gloves and masks for every home. We disinfect the places hands touch and the places germs live, like bathrooms and kitchen counters. We vacuum with our own vacuum, so you do not need to put anything out.

How long does it take, and what gets done?

Our Two Hour Clean is two people for two hours. In that time we focus on the kitchen, the bathrooms and the floors, the things you notice most. Want us to stay until a full checklist is done? Pick the Checklist Clean. We tell you how long we think it will take before you book.

What if I need more time?

At the end of checkout you can add an extra half hour. If our team has to leave for another home, we send you photos or a short video of anything left and you can ask us to finish it.

How much does it cost?

You see one flat price on the booking page before you pay. No surprise add-ons. Our goal is a fair price for regular homes, not a luxury price tag.

Do I have to sign up for a plan?

No. Book one clean, or book as often as you like. Recurring cleans cost a little less, and you can change or stop whenever you want. We are on demand: pick a day that works and we confirm it by text.

Is my home safe with you?

Yes. Our team is background checked and insured, and we have workers' compensation and liability coverage. Need proof of insurance for your building? Just ask and we will send the certificate.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many customers leave a key or a door code with us. We keep access details private and only share them with the team on your job.

What if I am not happy?

Tell us. Reply to our text or message us and we will make it right. We would rather fix it than lose you.

Where do you work?

We clean homes across the Twin Cities. Type your zip code on the booking page to see open times for your area.
